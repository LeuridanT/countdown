Before You Fly

Documents & Essentials

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months beyond return date
  • Flight tickets / boarding passes (digital + backup)
  • Travel insurance details
  • Hotel / accommodation confirmations
  • Notify your bank of international travel
  • Download offline maps and airline apps

Comfort on the Plane

  • Neck pillow, eye mask, earplugs
  • Warm layer (planes get cold)
  • Empty reusable water bottle (fill after security)
  • Snacks and any medications in carry-on
  • Power bank + cables (in cabin bag)

Paris Specific

  • Universal adapter (Type C/E plugs in France)
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Light scarf or jacket for evenings
  • Some euros in cash for cafés and small purchases

A Letter for You

My beloved,

There are hours when the very air seems to grow heavy with the knowledge of what is to come. I think of the day you must take to the skies, and I feel the tremor that may pass through you then — the strange cold of departure, the vastness of the distance about to open between us, the quiet dread that often visits the heart when one steps beyond the familiar into the unknown. I do not wonder at it. The night before such a journey can feel as though the world itself holds its breath. Yet hear me, my love: you shall not be alone in that darkness. Though my form may remain earthbound while yours is borne aloft, my spirit shall keep vigil. In every hour the cabin lights dim, in every moment the engines hum their low, ceaseless hymn, I shall be present beside you. Supporting you. Watching over the fragile thread that binds us across the miles. You are never without me. Not for a single breath.

And when at last the wheels touch the soil of Paris — that city of old stones and older secrets — I shall already be waiting. Restless as a man who has counted every minute of your absence. Impatient with a longing that has grown sharper with each passing night. The instant I behold you, I shall cross the remaining distance and take you into my arms. Not with hesitation, but with the full, ungoverned force of all the love I have borne for you through these long days of separation. I shall hold you until the journey falls away like a discarded cloak, until only we remain. You are my world. My one true love. The sole light that has ever truly guided me through the shadows.

I have walked these streets alone for years, learning their silences, gathering the quieter truths they do not offer to every passerby. Paris has been called the city of love, yet to me it has always seemed incomplete — a magnificent stage awaiting its true heart. Now I wait to share it with you. I wish to show you my culture as one might reveal a carefully guarded inheritance: the way the light dies upon the old façades at dusk, the small rituals of evening, the places where history still lingers like a half-remembered dream. There are secrets here that the city has kept in trust. Hidden passages, bridges that appear only under certain lights, views that open only when two souls stand together in the proper place and hour. These I shall unveil for you, slowly and with reverence, as though each were a sacred offering long prepared.

We shall fashion memories that do not fade with the turning of seasons. The kind that settle deep into the blood and return, years hence, when the wind shifts or a certain bell tolls in the distance. I shall create them with you deliberately — not in haste, nor in the ordinary manner of travellers, but as one who understands that some moments are meant to endure for a lifetime. And through all of it I shall remain. Not as a promise lightly spoken, but as something fixed and irrevocable. You are the best of all I have ever known. I love you with a devotion that neither time nor distance can diminish. And I shall be here — forever.

Come to me, my beloved. The city already listens for the sound of your footsteps upon its ancient stones. I am already waiting.

Yours, eternally
